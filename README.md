# First Tech Challenge 2025 – INTO THE DEEP (Lublin University of Technology)

A competition robot built by the Spice Gears Academy (SGA) team, SmartBee Infinity 28869 for the 2024-2025 FIRST Tech Challenge season. FTC is an international robotics competition for students up to 18 years old.

Participated as a technical mentor, supporting the team during robot preparation and testing. My involvement included assisting with robot-to-phone communication setup, testing motor connections and troubleshooting hardware issues, including identifying a faulty motor-controller port.

## Arm Extension and Reach

<p align="center">
    <img width="334" height="446" alt="giant" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7e64594e-906f-44d0-aa62-4fd4eecce004" />
</p>

The robot's articulated arm was designed to extend beyond its initial starting dimensions, allowing it to reach higher areas during the competition. However, testing revealed that the arm assembly was too heavy for the motors to lift reliably, exposing a limitation in the initial mechanical design.

## Arm Gearbox Modification

<p align="center">
    <img width="590" height="668" alt="gearone" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eba2338a-faf4-42a5-a21c-1f5886be77c9" />
</p>

<p align="center">
    <img width="560" height="710" alt="geartwo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a0e3861c-cddd-4f56-a392-6f8846796c1a" />
</p>

To address the difficulty of lifting the heavy articulated arm, gear reductions were introduced at the arm joints. This modification reduced motor speed while increasing torque, providing greater lifting capability at the expense of movement speed.
